## Landing-page image plan (no repeats)

The homepage draws every picture from one pool (`data/landingImages.ts`) and uses each picture once: hero rail (12), SDG cards (8), legacy block (1), enterprise cards (8) and field-news cards (6). `dedupeLandingSections()` enforces this at runtime so older database content with repeated pictures is corrected on screen.
